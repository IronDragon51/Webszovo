Trivium — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 July 2026

Trivium ("the app") is a trivia game published by Webszovo. We designed it to be private by default. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information.

The short version: Trivium collects nothing. There are no accounts, no logins, no ads, and no tracking. Everything you do stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts and requires no sign-up.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, Trivium saves the following locally on your device only (it never leaves your phone):

  • The nickname and avatar you choose
  • Your game progress: scores, streaks, level, coins, and settings

You can erase all of it at any time using Reset progress in the app's Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Internet & permissions

Trivium works fully offline — the question bank is built into the app. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Children

Trivium includes an optional Kids Mode. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either. It contains no ads and no external links designed to lead children away from the app.

Purchases

If an optional subscription ("Trivium+") is offered, payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
